Privacy Policy

Draft — needs legal review before launch. Written in plain English by the people who build Meetle, and accurate to how the product works today.

Last updated 19 September 2026.

The short version: we collect as little as we can, we never save your chats with strangers, and we don't sell anything about you to anyone.

The short version

  • This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party scripts. Fonts are hosted here.
  • The waitlist stores your email address so we can tell you when Meetle opens.
  • In the app, strangers see your username and a throwaway id. Nothing else.
  • Chats with strangers are never stored. Messages with matched friends are.
  • We don't sell or share your data. Email hello@meetle.org to delete it.

This website (meetle.org)

The site is static files. It doesn't set cookies, doesn't use analytics, and doesn't load anything from third parties. Our host, GitHub Pages, may keep standard server logs (IP address, page requested, time) for its own operations; see GitHub's privacy statement for that.

The waitlist

When you join the waitlist we store your email address and the time you joined, with the provider we use to hold the list. We use it for one thing: telling you when you can start talking, plus the occasional update about Meetle. We don't sell it, rent it or share it. Every email has an unsubscribe link, or you can reply "remove me". To have your email deleted from the list entirely, write to hello@meetle.org.

The app: what we store

  • Your account. You sign in with Google, Discord or GitHub through our sign-in provider, Supabase. We receive the email address on that account and an id from the provider, and when you signed up. We don't ask for your name, birthday, photo or location, and we don't post on your behalf.
  • Your username. The 3–20 character name you chose. It's the only thing other users see.
  • Your friends list. Who you've added and who you've matched with. Tapping Add friend is stored the moment you tap it — that's how a match can happen later — but it is never shown to the other person unless it becomes mutual. Removing a friend is stored too.
  • Direct Messages. Messages between you and a matched friend are saved so both of you can read them later. Only the two of you can read them. There is no way to edit or delete a sent message from inside the app yet; if you need messages removed, email us.
  • Reports. When you report someone we record when, the chat it happened in, and the reason if you gave one. Because stranger chats aren't stored, a report never includes the conversation.
  • Technical logs. Short-lived server logs (connection times, errors, IP addresses) that we use to keep Meetle running and to stop abuse such as flooding.

The app: what we never store

  • Chats with strangers. Messages between two people who aren't friends are relayed live through our server's memory and never written anywhere. When the chat ends, they're gone. This is a design decision, not a temporary one.
  • Your camera or microphone. Meetle doesn't ask for either.
  • Your location, contacts, or anything else on your device.
  • A profile. There's no bio, no photos, and no browsing other people's accounts.

What strangers can see

Your username and a temporary id like user_a83f91 that changes every time you connect. Never your name, email, provider, photo or account id.

Who we share it with

Nobody, apart from the services that store it for us: Supabase for accounts, friends and Direct Messages; GitHub Pages for this site; and the waitlist provider named above. We don't sell data, we don't share it with advertisers, and we'd only hand it over if the law required it.

How long we keep it

Waitlist email: until you unsubscribe, or until Meetle opens and the list is no longer needed. Account, username, friends and Direct Messages: until you delete your account. Reports: as long as needed to act on them. Technical logs: a short, fixed period.

Deleting your data

Email hello@meetle.org from the address on your account and we'll delete your account, username, friends list and Direct Messages, and remove you from the waitlist. We'll confirm when it's done.

Terms of Service →

Under 18

Meetle is for adults. We don't knowingly collect anything from anyone under 18, and we remove accounts that turn out to be. If you think someone under 18 has an account, email hello@meetle.org.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so on this page and, if you're on the waitlist or have an account, by email. The date at the top is the date of the last change.
