Omegle alternative

A text-only Omegle alternative, with real accounts

Credit where it's due

What Omegle got right

A real person, instantly. No sign-up, no profile, no waiting for someone to like you back. And a Next button, so no conversation ever had to be endured. From its launch in March 2009 that was enough to make "talk to strangers" a whole genre — and it's the part Meetle keeps.

What went wrong

Why Omegle shut down

Omegle closed on 8 November 2023, after years of abuse reports and a lawsuit it couldn't outrun. Nobody was accountable, so reporting someone meant nothing: Australia's eSafety Commissioner said the site ran with three moderators for video chat and one for text, against as many as 40,000 people online at once. The camera came first, so it turned into a place you'd rather not describe. And every conversation vanished — including the good ones. You'd have a great half-hour with someone and never be able to find them again.

Most of what replaced it is the same thing with worse ads: video first, no accounts, no age rule, and nothing that sticks when you hit Report.

Sources: Wikipedia "Omegle"; eSafety Commissioner statement, 9 Nov 2023; NPR, 9 Nov 2023.

Same spark, different rules

What we kept, what we changed

Kept

  • One stranger at a time, right now.
  • A Next button, no explanation needed.
  • No profile to build, no bio to write.
  • Nothing saved when the chat ends.

Changed

  • Everyone signs in (Google, Discord or GitHub): real accounts behind every name.
  • Text only. No camera, no microphone.
  • 18+ only, confirmed before every chat.
  • Add friend: keep the good ones — only if it's mutual.
  • Report ends the chat instantly.

No webcam required

Chat with strangers without video

Most "Omegle alternatives" are video first, which is exactly what people are trying to escape. On Meetle there's no camera to point at yourself, no room to tidy, no face to be judged by, and no microphone either. You can talk from anywhere, think for a second before you reply, and be read for what you actually say. It's the part of the internet that made friends, not the part that made screenshots.

The part Omegle never had

Keep the good ones

On Omegle a great conversation ended when someone's Wi-Fi did. On Meetle you tap Add friend. They're told nothing — unless they tap it too, and then you both see "You and QuietStorm matched as friends!" and the chat gets a permanent home in DMs. If they don't, nothing happens and nobody's the wiser. It's a random chat where the good conversations don't have to end — and the awkward ones never get awkward. Talk first, match later.

Be honest with yourself

Is Meetle for you?

Meetle is for you if

  • you want a real conversation with a real stranger, without a camera.
  • you're tired of profiles and swiping and just want to talk.
  • you'd like a way to keep the rare good one.
  • you're 18 or older.

Meetle isn't for you if

  • you want video or voice chat. There isn't any.
  • you want to stay fully anonymous with no sign-in. Meetle needs an account.
  • you're looking for a dating app with photos. There are no photos.
  • you're under 18.

Whichever site you pick

What to look for in an Omegle alternative

Before you trust any random chat site, check four things: does it require an account, is there a visible Report button and does it actually end the chat, is there an age rule, and does the site say whether it stores your chats. Meetle answers yes, yes, 18+, and never for stranger chats. The full list of what we do and don't do is on the Safety page.

Omegle questions

What happened to Omegle?

Omegle shut down on 8 November 2023. Its founder closed it after years of abuse reports and a lawsuit, saying the fight to keep it running wasn't sustainable. The site had run for fourteen years with almost no accountability: no accounts, cameras by default, and very few moderators.

Is Omegle coming back in 2026?

The original Omegle is not coming back. A teaser page has reportedly appeared at the old address under a new owner; if something launches there, it's a different service run by different people.

Is Omegle text chat still available anywhere?

Not the original. Several sites copied Omegle's video mode; far fewer kept the text-only spirit. Meetle is text first and text only: one random stranger, a Next button, and no camera at all.

What is the closest thing to Omegle's text chat?

A random one-on-one text chat with a Next button — which is exactly what Meetle is, with three differences: everyone signs in, everyone is 18+, and a good conversation can continue if you both tap Add friend.

Is there an Omegle alternative without video?

Yes. Meetle has no camera and no microphone — not as an option, at all. Strangers see your username and a throwaway id, never your face.

Are text-only chat sites safer than video chat?

Generally lower exposure: nobody can flash you, record you, or screenshot your room, and there's no face to be judged on. They aren't risk-free — still keep personal details to yourself, use Report, and prefer sites that require an account and say whether they store chats.

What should I look for in an Omegle alternative?

Four things: whether it requires an account (so reports stick to a person), a visible Report button that actually ends the chat, an age rule, and a plain statement about whether your chats are stored. Meetle: account required, Report ends the chat, 18+, stranger chats never stored.
