Omegle alternative

A text-first Omegle alternative, with real accounts

Credit where it's due

What Omegle got right

A real person, instantly. No sign-up, no profile, no waiting for someone to like you back. And a Next button, so no conversation ever had to be endured. From its launch in March 2009 that was enough to make "talk to strangers" a whole genre — and it's the part Meetle keeps.

What went wrong

Why Omegle shut down

Omegle closed on 8 November 2023, after years of abuse reports and a lawsuit it couldn't outrun. Nobody was accountable, so reporting someone meant nothing: Australia's eSafety Commissioner said the site ran with three moderators for video chat and one for text, against as many as 40,000 people online at once. The camera came first, so it turned into a place you'd rather not describe. And every conversation vanished — including the good ones. You'd have a great half-hour with someone and never be able to find them again.

Most of what replaced it is the same thing with worse ads: video first, no accounts, no age rule, and nothing that sticks when you hit Report.

Sources: Wikipedia "Omegle"; eSafety Commissioner statement, 10 Nov 2023; NPR, 9 Nov 2023.

Same spark, different rules

What we kept, what we changed

Kept

  • One stranger at a time, right now.
  • A way out in one tap, no explanation needed.
  • No profile to build, no bio to write.
  • Nothing saved when the conversation ends.

Changed

  • Everyone signs in (Google, Discord or GitHub): real accounts behind every name.
  • Text first. Voice and video only if you both choose them, and video starts covered.
  • 18+ only. You tick "I'm 18 or over" before you start.
  • Keep in touch: keep the good ones — only if it's mutual.
  • Report ends the conversation instantly.
  • Question cards and small games for the awkward first minute.

Camera optional, and covered

Chat with strangers without video

Most "Omegle alternatives" are video first, which is exactly what people are trying to escape. On Meetle every conversation starts in text, and it can stay there: nobody is looking at you, there's no room to tidy and no face to be judged by. You can talk from anywhere, think for a second before you reply, and be read for what you actually say.

If you both want more, you can both choose voice, or video. Video starts covered for both of you and uncovers only when you both tap — and either of you can cover it again, instantly.

The part Omegle never had

Keep the good ones

On Omegle a great conversation ended when someone's Wi-Fi did. On Meetle you tap Keep in touch. They're told nothing — unless they tap it too, and then you both see "You and maya_reads kept each other — you can talk again any time." and you get a place to keep talking, where what you say from then on is saved. If they don't, nothing happens and nobody's the wiser. It's a random chat where the good conversations don't have to end — and the awkward ones never get awkward.

Be honest with yourself

Is Meetle for you?

Meetle is for you if

  • you want a real conversation with a real stranger, without having to be on camera.
  • you're tired of profiles and swiping and just want to talk.
  • you'd like a way to keep the rare good one.
  • you're 18 or older.

Meetle isn't for you if

  • you want to go straight to camera with strangers. Here, text comes first and video starts covered.
  • you want to stay fully anonymous with no sign-in. Meetle needs an account.
  • you're looking for a dating app with photos. There are no photos.
  • you're under 18.

Whichever site you pick

What to look for in an Omegle alternative

Before you trust any random chat site, check five things: does it require an account, is there a visible Report button and does it actually end the chat, is there an age rule, does the site say whether it stores your chats, and if there's video, can a stranger see you before you agree. Meetle answers yes, yes, 18+, never for stranger chats, and no — video starts covered. The full list of what we do and don't do is on the Safety page.

Omegle questions

What happened to Omegle?

Omegle shut down on 8 November 2023. Its founder closed it after years of abuse reports and a lawsuit, saying the fight to keep it running wasn't sustainable. The site had run for fourteen years with almost no accountability: no accounts, cameras by default, and very few moderators.

Is Omegle coming back in 2026?

The original Omegle is not coming back. A teaser page has reportedly appeared at the old address under a new owner; if something launches there, it's a different service run by different people.

Is Omegle text chat still available anywhere?

Not the original. Several sites copied Omegle's video mode; far fewer kept the spirit of its text chat. Meetle starts every conversation in text: one random stranger, one tap to leave, and video only if you both choose it — starting covered.

What is the closest thing to Omegle's text chat?

A random one-on-one text chat with a way out in one tap — which is what Meetle is, with three differences: everyone signs in, it's 18+ only, and a good conversation can continue if you both tap Keep in touch.

Is there an Omegle alternative without video?

Yes. On Meetle every conversation starts in text, and it can stay that way for as long as you like. Video only happens if you both choose it, and even then it starts covered: nobody sees your face unless you both tap to uncover.

Is text chat safer than video chat?

Generally lower exposure: in text nobody can flash you, record your face, or screenshot your room, and there's no face to be judged on. It isn't risk-free — still keep personal details to yourself, use Report, and prefer sites that require an account and say whether they store chats. It's why Meetle starts every conversation in text and keeps video covered until you both uncover it.

What should I look for in an Omegle alternative?

Five things: whether it requires an account, a visible Report button that actually ends the chat, an age rule, a plain statement about whether your chats are stored, and — if there's video — whether a stranger can see you before you agree. Meetle: account required, Report ends the conversation, 18+, stranger chats never stored, video starts covered.
