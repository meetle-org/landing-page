The basics

What is Meetle?

Meetle is a random one-on-one text chat with strangers: you're paired with one person and talk first, and if you both tap Add friend you match and keep talking in DMs — if only one of you does, nothing happens and nobody finds out. You sign in with Google, Discord or GitHub, pick a username, and that's your whole profile. It's 18+ and text only — no camera.

What does "talk first, match later" mean?

It means the conversation comes before the decision. Everywhere else you match first — a photo, a swipe, a bio — and maybe talk later. On Meetle you talk to a stranger with no profile in the way, and you only become friends if you both choose it afterwards, by each tapping Add friend. The match is silent: nobody is asked, nobody is told, nobody is turned down.

Is Meetle free?

Yes. Sign in with Google, Discord or GitHub and you're in. No card, no plan, nothing to unlock. Joining the waitlist is free too.

Do I need an account to use Meetle? Why?

Yes. You sign in with Google, Discord or GitHub. We get the email on that account and store the username you choose; we don't ask for a name, birthday, photo or location. The account isn't there to make you jump through hoops — it's so a report has a real person to stick to, not a browser tab someone can just reopen. Strangers never see it.

When can I use Meetle?

Not quite yet. The app runs, but it isn't open to the public — we're letting people in from the waitlist in waves. Join it with your email and you'll get one message when it's your turn. We'd rather tell you a real date than a hopeful one, so we're not naming one.

Privacy and safety

Is Meetle anonymous?

To the people you chat with, yes: they see your username and a throwaway id like user_a83f91 that changes every time you connect, and nothing else — no email, no name, no photo, no account id. To Meetle, no: you sign in with a real account, which is what makes a report mean something. Think of it as pseudonymous, not anonymous.

Do I need a profile?

No. Your username (3–20 characters, letters, numbers, underscores) is your whole profile. There's no bio, no photos, no interests to pick and no page anyone can browse. The first thing a stranger learns about you is what you type.

Does Meetle save my chats?

Chats with strangers: never. They're relayed live through our server's memory and written nowhere — no history, no scrollback, nothing on disk. When the chat ends, it's gone. Direct Messages with matched friends: yes, those are saved so you can both pick up later. That's the only place Meetle keeps messages.

What happens when I report someone?

The chat ends immediately for both of you, the report is recorded, and you're put back in line for someone new. They just see that you left. Because we don't keep stranger chats, the report doesn't include the conversation. Nobody is banned yet — bans for accounts that keep getting reported are the next thing we're building.

Is Meetle 18+? Can teens use it?

Meetle is 18+ only. You confirm it before every chat, and the Start Chat button won't turn on until you do. It's a confirmation, not an ID check, and we're upfront about that — but it's a firm rule, and accounts found to be under 18 are removed. If you're under 18, Meetle isn't for you.

Is Meetle safe?

Safer than a blank anonymous box, by design. Everyone signs in with a real account and confirms they're 18+. Strangers only see a username. There's no camera. Stranger chats are never stored. Report ends a chat instantly, and Next or Stop get you out of any chat with no explanation. What we can't do is vet people before they talk to you — so keep your details to yourself until you'd share them in real life. The Safety page has the full picture.

Friends and matching

How does matching work on Meetle?

Tick the 18+ box, tap Start Chat, and you'll see "Looking for someone…" while we pair you with whoever's been waiting longest — no filters, no algorithm. You're in one chat at a time. Tap Next for a new stranger, Stop to leave, or Report to end the chat and record a report.

How do I become friends with someone on Meetle?

During or after a chat, tap Add friend. The other person is told nothing — their screen doesn't change. If they tap Add friend too, you both see "You and QuietStorm matched as friends!" and they appear in your Direct Messages, where the chat is private and saved. If they never tap it, nothing happens. There's no accepting or declining and no list of people waiting on you. It's mutual or it's nothing.

Will the other person know if I add them?

No. Nothing is shown to them unless it becomes mutual. Their button still says Add friend, exactly as if you'd never tapped yours — and you'll never know whether they tapped theirs unless you both did.

Can I remove a friend?

Yes. Open the conversation and tap Remove friend. They drop out of your Direct Messages and you drop out of theirs. No message is sent, no drama.

Meetle and everything else

Does Meetle have video or voice chat?

No. Meetle is text only, and that's a decision, not a gap: no camera means nobody gets flashed, nobody's judged on their face or their room, and you can talk from anywhere. If you want video chat, Meetle isn't it.

Can I use Meetle on my phone? Do I need to download an app?

Meetle runs in your browser, on your phone or your computer. Nothing to install.

Is Meetle like Omegle?

In the good way. Like Omegle, you're paired with one random stranger and the chat is the whole thing — no profiles, no setup. Unlike Omegle, there's no video, everyone signs in with a real account, everyone's 18+, and a great chat doesn't have to end: tap Add friend, and if they do too, you're friends. There's a whole page on Meetle as a text-only Omegle alternative.

Is Meetle the same as the "Meetle: Video Chat & Meet" app on Google Play?

No. That Android app is not affiliated with us. Meetle at meetle.org is text only, requires sign-in, and does not have a video mode. There's no Meetle app to download — it runs in your browser.
