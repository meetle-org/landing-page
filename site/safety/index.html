Safety on Meetle

Meetle is built to be a safer random chat: everyone signs in with a real account, everyone is 18 or older, strangers see only your username, nothing you say to a stranger is saved, and Report ends a chat instantly. No random chat is risk-free — here's exactly what we do, what we don't, and what you can do.

What we do

Everyone is signed in

There are no throwaway accounts on Meetle. You sign in with Google, Discord or GitHub. That isn't to make you jump through hoops — it's so that when someone gets reported there's a real account behind the username, not a browser tab someone can just reopen. What you don't have to do is build a profile. No photos, no bio, nothing to fake and nothing to be judged on.

18 or older

You confirm you're 18 or older before the Start Chat button turns on, every time. We'll be straight with you: it's a confirmation, not an ID check. We don't verify identity, and we won't pretend we do. It's a firm rule all the same, and accounts found to belong to someone under 18 will be removed. If someone tells you they're under 18, end the chat and report them.

What strangers see

A username and a throwaway id

During a chat the other person sees two things: your username and a temporary id like user_a83f91 that changes every time you connect. Your email, your sign-in provider and your account id are never shown to anyone. There's no way to look you up, search for you, or message you again unless you've matched as friends. There is nothing on Meetle to catfish with, because there's no picture to fake.

Report

One tap ends it

Tap Report and three things happen at once: the chat ends for both of you, the report is recorded, and you're put back in line for someone new. You don't have to explain, argue or wait. The person you reported just sees that you left, and they can't find you again. Every account is signed in, so a report has somewhere to stick — bans for accounts that keep getting reported are the next thing we're building. Next and Stop are always there too: you never need a reason to leave.

Nothing saved

When it's over, it's gone

Stranger chats are never stored. They pass through our server in memory and are gone when the chat ends — not in a database, not "for moderation", not for training anything. That means nobody, not another user and not us, can pull up what you said to a stranger last night. The one exception is on purpose: messages with a matched friend are saved so you can both read them later.

Built in

The boring, invisible stuff

  • Messages cap at 1,000 characters and are rate-limited, so nobody can flood your screen.
  • Pairing happens on the server. Nobody can pick who they get matched with, be in two chats with you at once, or message you by id.
  • Nothing on your screen ever ties a stranger back to their real account, and nothing on theirs ties back to yours.
  • Next and Stop are always one tap away. Leaving is normal here.

Your part

Five things worth remembering

  1. Keep it in Meetle. Don't move to another app until you'd hand this person your number in real life.
  2. Your username is enough. No last name, school, workplace, address, or the name of your street. Friends can earn that later, in DMs, at your pace.
  3. Nobody here needs your money. No gift cards, no crypto, no "verification codes". Anyone who asks is running a scam. Report them.
  4. Trust the weird feeling. Next costs nothing.
  5. Meeting a friend in real life is a big step. Public place, daytime, tell someone.

The long version is in the Terms.

Honestly

Being honest about the gaps

Meetle is new. Right now a report ends the chat and is recorded — nobody is banned yet, and nobody reads stranger chats, because they aren't stored. We're a small team, not a moderation department. Bans for repeatedly reported accounts are the next thing we're building; automatic moderation inside live chats is after that. We'll say what's live on this page, and we won't promise dates. If something on Meetle worried you and Report wasn't enough, email hello@meetle.org — a person reads it.
